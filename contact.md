### What I'm Open To

* **Delivery & Programme Roles:** I am open to senior delivery, product, and programme leadership positions. If you are hiring, a short note on the role and the challenge it exists to solve is the best place to start.
* **Advisory & Consulting:** If you are navigating an infrastructure modernisation, a complex rollout, or need rigorous delivery governance, include a brief summary of your timeline and current friction points so I can be useful quickly.
* **Mentorship:** I reserve dedicated time to mentor university students and early-career professionals in project management, business analysis, and economics. Lead with your current career stage and the specific hurdles you are trying to clear.
* **Keynote Speaking:** I speak on delivery and ways of working, personal development, and self-directed learning. Share the event, audience, and date, and I will let you know if it is a fit.
* **Anything Else:** Happy to talk shop on self-hosted infrastructure, Docker stacks, or language-learning frameworks any time.
