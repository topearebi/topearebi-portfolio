Replacing a mission-critical legacy system is akin to changing the engine of an aircraft while it is still in flight. The Timetabling Replacement (TTR) System required a comprehensive end-to-end decommissioning of outdated infrastructure and the simultaneous rollout of a high-availability, modernized engine—all without disrupting daily institutional operations.

As the Workstream Modernisation Lead, the mandate was clear: zero service interruption.

### Synchronizing Academic and Operational Delivery

The technical hurdles of data migration were secondary to the logistical complexities of alignment. A timetabling system touches every academic and operational department, meaning the deployment parameters had to be synchronized across highly distinct stakeholder groups with competing priorities.

The strategy relied on:
* **Phased Decommissioning:** Rather than a hard cutover, we architected a staged transition, running concurrent validation checks between the legacy environment and the new TTR engine to guarantee data fidelity.
* **Cross-Departmental Synchronization:** Establishing strict, transparent delivery gates. Operational departments needed to know exactly when system downtime would occur, down to the minute, to manage their own local continuity plans.

Modernization is only successful if the business can continue to function during the transition. By prioritizing architectural redundancy and stakeholder alignment, we achieved a seamless cutover to a highly resilient platform.
