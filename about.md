At my core, I am a problem chaser. I have always pursued hard problems rather than streamlined career paths. My natural disposition as a systems thinker makes it easy for me to apply first-principles thinking and structured problem solving across a wide array of fields. My professional career is defined by leading complex digital transformations and owning enterprise delivery and governance — but that same drive for optimisation, structure, and continuous improvement bleeds into every aspect of my life.

I lead delivery the way I think: end to end. I am accountable for outcomes, I advise the people who make the decisions, and I am primarily driven by the goal of making people's lives easier through efficiency — whether I am de-risking a multi-million-pound programme, writing a board report, or designing user-centric software requirements.

Over the past several years I have directed digital transformations across higher education, construction technology, and high-growth startups — governing multi-million-pound portfolios at the University of Salford, rebuilding a venture-backed SaaS platform into a Techstars '23 company, and launching products that captured real market share at Decagon Institute. I hold a PRINCE2 Practitioner certification, an MSc in Managing Innovation & Information Technology, and an MSc in Human Resource Management, alongside Scrum Master and Product Owner credentials.

### Beyond the Work

I believe in minimising decision fatigue to maximise execution. Whether it is applying a uniform wardrobe strategy for daily commutes or engineering my own personal digital infrastructure, I am constantly looking for ways to build resilient, high-leverage systems. That same instinct shapes how I spend my time outside of work, which is structured around physical endurance, language, and technology.

* **Athletics & Physical Discipline:** I maintain a high-intensity training regime — calisthenics, indoor bouldering, and 5k Parkruns. I view physical training, specifically time under tension and aerobic endurance, as the foundational bedrock for professional stamina.
* **Linguistics & Co-Learning:** I am a dedicated polyglot. Beyond native fluency in English and Yoruba, I am training toward HSK 3 in Mandarin and a B2 level in French. I also incorporate bilingual environmental labelling at home to raise my daughter in a multilingual ecosystem.
* **Self-Hosting & Homelab Infrastructure:** I prefer to own my digital footprint. I run a self-hosted VPS environment using Docker, Tailscale, and Caddy — building and troubleshooting my own server infrastructure to host utility and media applications, and to keep my technical skills sharp outside of the boardroom.
* **Literature:** I curate a meticulously organised cold-storage digital library, focused on tracking down and reading the best books in the world across the many disciplines of human life.
